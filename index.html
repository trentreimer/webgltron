How to play. You are a lightcycle. You cannot stop and you cannot slow down. Your trail is solid: touch any trail — including your own — or the arena wall and you derezz. Outlast every opponent to win the round. Crossing an opponent's trail scores you a point.

Booster. Hold boost to accelerate, release to bleed speed. The booster regenerates. Glance turns your head without turning the bike.
A recreation of gltron 0.70 (GPL) — the arena rules, lightcycle physics, computer opponents and camera modes are ported from the original sources by Andreas Umbach, Michael Kircher and contributors.
